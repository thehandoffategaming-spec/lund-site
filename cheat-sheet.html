Rules Cheat Sheet

A tri-fold quick reference for new players. Everything needed to take a first turn.

Print double-sided on Letter paper, landscape, flipping on the short edge, with margins set to None and background graphics on. Then fold into thirds: the Cover panel faces out.

Page 1 — Inside

Your Turn

  • A turn is a Move and an Action, in either order. A Move goes to an adjacent zone, or repositions you inside your zone.
  • Actions: typically Attack or cast a Spell, but anything goes. The GM rules on other Actions by context.
  • No initiative. All players act first, then the enemies.
  • Engaged means within touch distance in the same zone, with an ally or a foe. While Engaged with a foe, you can only leave if you still have Guard, or an ally Engaged with that foe helps cover you. Even then the GM may say no (nowhere to go, or you're overwhelmed). A Move Gambit always gets you out.

Making an Attack

Take the dice listed on your weapons, shield, and bonuses. Then, in order:

  1. Everyone attacking the same target rolls at the same time.
  2. The target (or a nearby ally) may Deny.
  3. Attackers may spend dice on Gambits.
  4. Take the highest die left.
  5. Add Bolster damage from allies.
  6. Subtract the target's Armor.
  7. The result is the Damage.

Attack Modifiers

  • Impaired: roll a single d4 only; no bonus dice, no Feats.
  • Enhanced: gain a bonus die (a d8 unless stated).
  • Blast (Small): pick a target; hits it and everyone Engaged with it. Blast (Large): hits everyone in a zone. Roll each separately.
  • Bombs: thrown at Long-Range; Blast (Small) unless stated.
  • Range: you can only target within the weapon's range. A ranged weapon or spell cannot Attack a foe Engaged with you unless it is Short-Range, Touch, or a Blast (Small) centered on you.
Example. A mace (d8), a shield (d4), and an ally with two daggers (d6 each) roll 7, 3, 1, 5. Highest is 7. The 5 is spent to Bolster: Damage 8. Target Armor 2 leaves 6 Damage.

Gambits

Discard any Attack dice showing 4 or higher. Each die buys one effect. The foe must be a target of your Attack. Except Bolster and Move, the target gets a Vigor Save to ignore it.

  • Bolster: +1 total Damage.
  • Move after the Attack, even if you already moved.
  • Repel a foe away from you.
  • Stop a foe from moving next turn.
  • Impair a foe's weapon or spellcasting next turn.
  • Trap a shield, or silence a caster, until your next turn.
  • Dismount a foe or disable a vehicle until your next turn.
  • Another effect of similar impact.

Strong Gambit

If a die of 8 or higher is used in any Attack (melee, ranged, or spell), choose one: no Save for the target, or a greater effect (disarm, break a wooden shield or weapon, remove a helm). A greater effect does not raise Bolster.

Damage

  1. Damage comes off Guard. At least 1 Guard left: you Evade.
  2. Exactly 0 Guard left: you gain a Scar.
  3. Excess past Guard comes off Vigor: you are Wounded.
  4. Lose half or more of your remaining Vigor: Mortal Wound. Down and dying; dead in an hour unless tended, but can be patched up in moments.
  5. Vigor reaches 0: Slain.

Scars

  • Gained when an Attack leaves you at exactly 0 Guard in real, deadly combat.
  • Re-roll the die that caused it, add your current Scar count, and read the Scar table in the Rules.
  • Effects clear at season's end or from a specialist. The tally never resets. Every 3 Scars grants 1 Experience.
  • Magical Scars come from spell Attacks that leave you at 0 Guard, or from spells that say so. They use their own table.

Inventory

  • 10 Item slots. Fill the tenth and you are Encumbered: Guard set to 0.
  • Worn gear takes no slot, except Bulky items, which take one.
  • Wear up to four armor pieces: Shield, Helm, Underlayer (such as a Gambeson), Outer Layer (such as Plate).
  • Petty items take no slot. Bombs, Flasks, Vials, and Syringes store three per slot. Remedies take a full slot each.

Spellcasting

  • Casting is an Action. Morph (yours first, then others) is applied before the spell resolves.
  • Then pay the cost from Spirit unless the spell says otherwise. Costs are 0, 2, 4, or 8.
  • Overflow: cost beyond your Spirit comes out of Vigor. If Vigor was already the casting Virtue, you suffer a Mortal Wound.
  • Spells take effect at once and rarely allow a Save, only when the target would be disabled, helpless, or wholly ineffective.
  • You can hold 10 spells in memory.

Ranges

Self · Touch (Engaged) · Medium (your zone) · Long (adjacent zones too) · Extreme (line of sight)

Spell Qualities

  • Attack: uses the Attacking rules with the spell's dice.
  • Virtue Loss: lowers a Virtue as stated, ignoring Guard and Armor. Not an Attack, and never Damage.
  • Blast: Small or Large, as under Attack Modifiers. Morph adds Blast (Small).
  • Curse: lasts until the caster is killed or a specialist removes it. One instance per caster at a time.

Fatigue & Rest

  • Short Rest (minutes): restores Guard, removes Fatigue.
  • Night's Rest (safe place, food, lodging): restores Vigor to full.
  • Full Rest (a week, safe): restores all Virtues.
  • Remedy: an hour, serves up to four Fatekin, restores 6 points of its Virtue.

Quick Rules for New Fatekin

The One Rule

Roll a d20. If it is equal to or under the Virtue being tested, you pass. Rolling low is good.

Virtues

VirtueStands forAt 0
VigorStrong body, sturdy bones, mighty heartExhausted: cannot Attack if you moved
GraceKeen thought, swift reflex, flawless motionImpaired: Attacks roll a single d4
SpiritComposed mind, striking presence, guarded soulExposed: Guard set to 0

Virtues range 0 to 19. Each is rolled 1d6 + 1d12.

Virtue Loss

Loss that is not Damage (spell costs, Scars, Fatigue effects). It can never Mortally Wound or Slay.

Guard & Armor

  • Guard is a pool (rolled 1d6). Damage comes off Guard before any Virtue. While you have at least 1 Guard, you Evade the hit.
  • Armor reduces Damage before it reaches Guard.
  • Without Guard, you cannot leave a foe you are Engaged with unless an ally assists your retreat.

Saves & Checks

  • Save (resist something) or Check (attempt something): roll d20, equal to or under the Virtue passes. Failure means a consequence, not always a failed action.
  • Opposed Check: the lower roll wins, but only if it is at or under the Virtue. Whoever has the most to lose wins ties.
  • Advantage: roll 2d20, keep the lower. Disadvantage: roll 2d20, keep the higher.
