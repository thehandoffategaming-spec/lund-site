4.1

Overview

Lund is the magical hub of innovation in spellcasting — every wizard's dream made real, and Lund knows it. Magic suffuses everything here. It makes life easier. It makes life bearable. For those who have it.

Those without magic are second-class citizens. Those without magic and without money are lower still. It is not a kind city to be poor in, and Lund does not pretend otherwise.

Every Fatekin begins play carrying a significant debt that must be repaid — it's the reason most of them picked up a weapon or a spellbook in the first place. Adventuring is, by a wide margin, the most lucrative work in town.

4.2

The City

Lund is circular, walled, and airborne — a flying island propelled by magic, ringed by an enormous wall, drifting over what seems to be an endless ocean on its home plane.

Eight major streets run the full width of the city, each crossing straight through its center like spokes on a wheel — though the city has its own word for the wedge-shaped districts those streets create. See The Spokes, below.

Lund only ever built up, because it couldn't build out. A walled island has nowhere to sprawl — so every generation simply built on top of the last one, and never looked back down to see what got left behind.

4.4

The Spokes

The wedge of the city between any two adjacent streets is called a Spoke. Each Spoke runs its own local government, headed by a body of officials called Spokesmen — and each Spoke writes and enforces its own laws, sometimes in sharp contrast to the Spoke next door.

Birth Spoke vs. Residential Spoke

Every citizen is born into a Spoke, and that birth Spoke stays on record for life — but most citizens also carry a current residential Spoke, if they've moved. Both are tracked, and both matter to whoever's asking.

4.5

The Lundercity

Every generation Lund built upward instead of outward, and every generation left something behind rather than tear it down. What's left is a labyrinth of fractured, abandoned structures buried beneath the city proper — colloquially, the Lundercity. It holds every piece of Lund's history the surface would rather forget.

The Lundercity is exempt from the Laws of Lund. No Spoke's Spokesmen claim it, no writ reaches it, and no tattoo tracks anyone who disappears into it. Naturally, it's become home to a thriving assortment of criminal enterprises and organizations that the city above finds it easier to ignore than to root out.

4.6

Lake Arcane

At the exact center of Lund sits the Lake Arcane, a magically generated source of pristinely clean water and the primary water supply for the entire city. By any practical measure it's also a national park — one of the only stretches of open water and greenery Lund has left.

It is also fiercely guarded. A wall rings the lake as surely as one rings the city, and admission is limited to citizens whose residential Spoke happens to be a Park Allowed Spoke. Everyone else drinks what's piped out to them and looks at the water from a distance.

4.7

The Floating Colleges

Scattered across the sky above Lund — and clustered thickest above the Lake Arcane — are freestanding structures that function as colleges, teaching the magic the whole city runs on. The most prestigious of them floats directly above the Lake Arcane itself: the College of Enlightenment.

4.8

Citizenship & Debt

Every citizen of Lund bears an identification stamp — a magically inscribed tattoo on the forearm, recording everything the city considers worth keeping tabs on: birth Spoke, residential Spoke, and whatever else a given official is entitled to read off it.

Debt is the other constant. Every Fatekin starts with one, measured the same way everything else in Lund is measured — in Xchange-Credit. Paying it down is rarely fast, and almost never gentle, which is exactly why the adventuring trade exists at all.
