Weapon Qualities

Hefty

The weapon can be wielded with one hand, but you cannot hold two in either hand.

Bulky

Takes up an additional inventory slot. If it's a weapon, it also requires two hands to use.

Long

The weapon is Impaired when used in tight spaces, and it requires two hands to use.

Blast

The weapon can be used to make Blast attacks naturally.

Short-Range

A quality of ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of up to 10 meters.

Long-Range

A quality of ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of up to 100 meters.

Extreme-Range

A quality of ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of up to 500 meters.

Close-Range

A quality of some ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of touch, wihtin a meter.

Slow

Cannot attack and move in the same turn with a slow weapon.

Fixed

Requires at least one turn of set-up before use.
