Item Tables

Everything a character might carry, buy, or find — weapons, armor, and the volatile little surprises sold out of a satchel. Costs are listed in Xchange-Credit, shorthand x.

Weapon Qualities

Hefty

The weapon can be wielded with one hand, but you cannot hold two in either hand.

Bulky

Takes up an additional inventory slot. If it's a weapon, it also requires two hands to use.

Long

The weapon is Impaired when used in tight spaces, and it requires two hands to use.

Blast

The weapon can be used to make Blast attacks naturally.

Short-Range

A quality of some ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of touch, within a meter.

Medium-Range

A quality of ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of up to 10 meters.

Long-Range

A quality of ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of up to 100 meters.

Extreme-Range

A quality of ranged weapons, denoting an effective range of up to 500 meters.

Slow

Cannot attack and move in the same turn with a slow weapon.

Fixed

Requires at least one turn of set-up before use.

Hidden

Can be easily hidden on your person, avoiding detection without a Check.

Armor Qualities

Underlayer

Armor worn beneath other armor. You can wear one Underlayer and one Outerlayer at the same time, and both grant their Armor.

Outerlayer

Armor worn over other armor. You can wear one Underlayer and one Outerlayer at the same time, and both grant their Armor.

Helmet

Armor worn on the head. It is its own armor category, neither Underlayer nor Outerlayer, so it stacks with both.

Shield

Armor carried in the hand. It is its own armor category, neither Underlayer nor Outerlayer, so it stacks with both.

Oddities

Oddities are the magic items of Lund: unlabeled, unfinished, and rarely reliable. Each takes one inventory slot, and using one is an Action unless it says otherwise.

Most Oddities carry a Quirk, a side effect the GM triggers whenever the Oddity is used, or handled carelessly. An unknown Oddity can be studied for an hour to reveal its effect and Quirk, and handling one blind may set it off. Roll a die, or let the GM choose.

Learning New Spells

Spells that do not come from a Fate or from advancing can be learned in two ways: from a spell-bearing Oddity, as above, or from a teacher, a text, or careful experiment.

Studying a spell costs a teacher's or library's fee of 200x per point of casting cost, with a minimum of 200x (so even a free spell costs 200x). The time it takes depends on the spell's tier: a Full Rest for a free or 1st tier spell (cost 1 or 2), a season for a 2nd tier spell (cost 4), and an age for a 3rd tier spell (cost 8). When the study is done, the spell enters your memory. No Check is needed. A character can only hold ten spells in memory at once.

Augments

Augments are body and mind modifications installed by Lund's specialists. Each one has a price and a permanent Virtue Loss, listed on its entry. A Full Rest does not restore that loss, and neither does removing the augment.

A Fatekin can carry up to three augments in total, Body and Mind combined. Installing one takes a Full Rest in the specialist's care, and each augment can only be installed once per Fatekin. Because the cost is Virtue Loss and not Damage, it can never Mortally Wound or Slay.
