Chapter V

Character Generation Tools

From first breath to first adventure — follow the steps below, roll your Virtues and Guard, then draw your Fate to find the thread your story starts on.

1

Roll Your Virtues & Guard

Choose a starting level if your GM allows it, then use the roller below — roll three dice pools and assign them to VIGOR, GRACE, and SPIRIT as you like, then roll GUARD.

2

Draw Your Starting Fate

Draw three cards below and choose one to keep as your starting Fate. Its suit (Wands, Cups, Swords, or Pentacles) determines which Feats and spells you can choose from next.

Each Fate offers a Spell or a Gift — choose one, not both. Its Starting Gear only comes with you if this card is your starting Fate at Level 1; a Fate kept later, from advancement, grants just the Spell or Gift.

3

Choose 2 Feats or Spells

Pick two total from your Fate's suit, drawn from the Feats in Chapter I and the spell list in Chapter III — two Feats, two spells, or one of each. Deny and Focus are universal Feats, open to any Fatekin regardless of suit.

4

Select Starting Gear

Every Fatekin starts with 300x to spend from the Common Items list in Chapter II, if they wish. Your Fate's suit also grants a bonus gift on top of whatever you buy:

  • Wands — an extra spell from the Wands spell list
  • Cups — 3x Uncommon Consumables (Bombs, Remedies, or Poisons)
  • Swords — a Common Weapon, Shield, or Helm
  • Pentacles — an extra 300x
