# Lund TTRPG: Rules Reference (paste into a chat or add as an artifact)

Use this as the authoritative rules for the "Lund" tabletop RPG. Players are **Fatekin** in the city of **Lund**. Rolling LOW is good. Spells, items, and Fates live in separate data (Chapters II and III); this file covers the core rules (Chapter I). If a rule isn't here, say so rather than inventing one.

## 1. Characters
- Three **Virtues**: **Vigor** (strong body, sturdy bones, mighty heart), **Grace** (keen thought, swift reflex, flawless motion), **Spirit** (composed mind, striking presence, guarded soul). Level 1: roll 1d6 + 1d12 three times and assign freely. Virtues range **0 to 19**.
- **Guard**: a separate pool, rolled 1d6 at Level 1. Damage comes off Guard before any Virtue.
- **Armor** reduces Damage *before* it reaches Guard.
- **Virtues at 0:** Vigor 0 = **Exhausted** (cannot Attack if you moved this turn). Grace 0 = **Impaired** (Attacks roll a single d4). Spirit 0 = **Exposed** (Guard set to 0).
- **Levels:** L2 gains: draw 3 Minor Arcana and keep 1, roll 2d6 Guard (replace if higher), +1 to two Virtues. L3 gains: draw 3 Major Arcana and keep 1, roll 3d6 Guard (replace if higher), +2 to two Virtues. Starting at higher levels: L2 uses 3d6 per Virtue (x3) and 2d6 Guard, +1 to any 2 Virtues; L3 uses 2d6+5 per Virtue (x3) and 2d6+6 Guard, +2 to any 2 Virtues.

## 2. Saves, Checks, Virtue Loss
- **Save or Check:** roll a d20. Equal to or under the relevant Virtue passes. Failure has consequences, not always a failed action.
- **Opposed Check:** lower roll wins, but only if it is at or under the Virtue. Whoever has the most to lose wins ties.
- **Advantage:** roll 2d20, keep the **lower**. **Disadvantage:** roll 2d20, keep the **higher**.
- **Virtue Loss:** any reduction of a Virtue that is not Damage (spell costs, some spells, Scars, hazards). It ignores Guard and Armor, and can **never Mortally Wound or Slay**.

## 3. Turns and Zones
- A turn = a **Move** and an **Action**, in either order. No individual initiative: all players act first, then enemies. Actions are not fully codified: typically Attack or cast a Spell, but anything goes and the GM rules on others by context. There is no "Help" action.
- **Zone:** a loose area (a room, a courtyard, a street stretch, one level) drawn by the GM. Zones are **adjacent** when the fiction connects them (door, window, stair, gap, ledge). The GM decides adjacency from context.
- **Engaged:** within touch distance, inside one zone, with any number of allies or foes. Melee Attacks and Touch spells target Engaged creatures.
- **Move:** either to an **adjacent zone** (choose your position there, Engaged with whom you like or no one) or **reposition within your zone** (engage or disengage freely).
- **Leaving Engaged:** you cannot disengage or leave the zone unless you still have **Guard**, or an ally Engaged with that enemy covers you. Even with Guard the GM may say no (nowhere to go, overwhelmed). A **Move Gambit** always gets you out.
- **Short distance:** a repositioning within a zone (being pushed or pulled into or out of Engaged).
- **Zone features:** most zones have a GM-chosen feature (river, trapped hall, sniper hill). **Difficult terrain** costs **2 Moves** to leave; the Moves can be split across turns, progress is kept, a Move Gambit counts as one of the two, and being Repelled or pulled out is free. Zones can also have hazards (Damage, Virtue Loss, Saves, conditions on enter, end turn, or leave), obstacles and cover, and high ground or chokepoints.
- **Ranges:** **Touch** = a target Engaged with you. **Close-Range** = anywhere in your zone. **Medium-Range** = your zone or an adjacent zone you can see. **Long-Range** = anywhere in line of sight. (Spells also have Self.) **Stretching a shot:** when attacking with a ranged weapon, remove one die from the Attack before rolling to add 1 zone of base range (each extra die adds another zone; Touch then reaches your zone, Close an adjacent zone, Medium two zones away; Long gains nothing). Outside combat, real distances still apply.

## 4. Attacking
Take the Attack dice from your weapons, shield, and bonuses, then in order:
1. Everyone attacking the same target **rolls at once**.
2. The target or a nearby ally may **Deny**.
3. Attackers may spend dice on **Gambits**.
4. Take the **highest** remaining die.
5. Add **Bolster** damage from allies.
6. Subtract the target's **Armor**.
7. The result is the **Damage**.

Example: mace d8 + shield d4 + an ally's two d6 roll 7, 3, 1, 5. Highest 7; the 5 is spent to Bolster (+1): Damage 8; Armor 2 leaves 6.

- **Impaired:** roll a single d4 only, no bonus dice, no Feats.
- **Enhanced:** gain a bonus die (a d8 unless stated).
- **Blast (Small):** pick a target; the Attack hits it and every creature Engaged with it. **Blast (Large):** hits every creature in a single zone. Roll each target separately.
- **Bombs:** thrown at Medium-Range; Blast (Small) unless stated.
- **Range limit:** an Attack can only target within its range. A ranged weapon or spell **cannot** Attack an enemy Engaged with you unless it has Touch range or is a Blast (Small) centered on you.

## 5. Gambits
Discard any Attack dice showing **4 or higher**; each buys one effect on a target of the Attack. Except Bolster and Move, the target gets a **Vigor Save** to ignore it.
- **Bolster:** +1 total Damage.
- **Move** after the Attack, even if you already moved or can't. Always frees you from Engaged foes.
- **Repel:** break Engaged by pushing a foe a short distance within the zone; into another zone only if it makes sense (ledge, doorway, great force).
- **Stop** a foe from moving next turn. **Impair** a foe's weapon or spellcasting next turn. **Trap** a shield or **silence** a caster until your next turn. **Dismount** a foe or disable a vehicle until your next turn. Other effects of similar impact.
- **Strong Gambit:** if a die of **8 or higher** is used in any Attack (including ranged and spells), choose: **no Save** for the target, or a **greater effect** (disarm, break a wooden shield or weapon, remove a helm, hurl a foe into an adjacent zone); a greater effect does not raise Bolster.

## 6. Damage, Wounds, Scars
- Damage comes off **Guard**. At least 1 Guard left: you **Evade**. Exactly 0 Guard left: you gain a **Scar**. Excess past Guard comes off **Vigor**: you are **Wounded**.
- Losing **half or more of your remaining Vigor** in one hit is a **Mortal Wound**: down and dying, dead after an hour untended, but can be patched up in a few moments. Vigor 0 from Damage = **Slain**.
- **Scars:** only from real deadly combat. Re-roll the die that caused it, **add your current Scar count**, and read the table. Effects clear at the season's end or with a specialist; the tally is permanent. Every 3 Scars = 1 Experience. Scar Virtue Loss is not Damage.

## 7. Feats
Special actions. Using a Feat risks **Fatigue** (no further Feats until a Short Rest). Each Feat once per Attack per combatant. After use, pass the listed Save or become Fatigued. **Deny** and **Focus** are universal; the rest belong to an Arcana suit.

| Feat | Suit | Effect | Save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deny | Universal | After an Attack roll against you or an ally Engaged with you, discard one Attack die | Grace |
| Focus | Universal | After rolling an Attack, perform a Gambit without spending a die | Spirit |
| Divine Smite | Pentacles | Before a melee Attack without Blast: it gains +1d12 | Vigor |
| Cleave | Swords | Before a melee Attack: it gains Blast (Small) centered on you, hitting every creature Engaged with you or in reach of your weapon | Vigor |
| Masterstrike | Swords | Before an Attack without Blast: it ignores Armor | Vigor |
| Morph | Wands | When you see a spell cast: ignore allies in Blast, add or remove Blast (added Blast is Small), change cost, change damage type, add or remove a target, or cast without sound or gesture | Spirit |
| Humiliate | Cups | When dealing Damage, choose which Virtue is damaged | Spirit |
| Ruse | Cups | Before an Attack, make two Attacks and pick one | Grace |

## 8. Spellcasting
- Casting is an **Action**. Morph (caster's first, then others') applies before the spell resolves; a Morph effect can't be applied twice, and opposing effects cancel.
- Then pay the **cost** from **Spirit** (unless stated), typically 0, 2, 4, or 8. **Overflow:** any excess comes out of **Vigor**. If Vigor was already the casting Virtue, the caster suffers a **Mortal Wound**.
- Spells take effect at once and rarely allow a Save (only when the target would be disabled, helpless, or wholly ineffective). Hold up to **10 spells** in memory.
- **Ranges:** Self; Touch (Engaged); Close-Range; Medium-Range; Long-Range.
- **Qualities:** **Attack** (uses the Attacking rules with the spell's dice); **Virtue Loss** (lowers a Virtue as stated, no Guard or Armor, never Damage); **Save** (defender saves with their own Virtue); **Target**; **Blast** (Small or Large, as in Attacking; Morph adds Blast (Small)); **Curse** (lasts until the caster is killed or a specialist removes it; one instance per caster at a time, covering all targets from that casting).
- When casting a Blast spell the caster picks a creature as its target for Morph's purposes (any creature in the zone for Large); it still casts if none is present.

## 9. Conditions
Fatigued (no Feats until rest) · Exhausted (cannot Attack if you moved) · Exposed (Guard 0) · Impaired (Attacks roll a single d4, no bonus dice or Feats) · Vulnerable (attacks against you are Enhanced) · Deprived (cannot benefit from resting) · Dazed (cannot perform Gambits) · Bound (cannot move) · Shaken (Disadvantage on Spirit Checks and Saves until the source is removed). Conditions stack unless noted and don't clear automatically at the end of combat.

## 10. Inventory
- **10 Item slots** and 10 Spell slots. Fill the tenth Item slot and you are **Encumbered**: Guard 0.
- Worn gear takes no slot, except **Bulky** items (one slot even when worn).
- Armor: wear up to four pieces at once: **Shield**, **Helm**, **Underlayer** (e.g. Gambeson), **Outerlayer** (e.g. Plate). Shield and Helm are their own categories and stack with both layers.
- **Petty** items take no slot. Bombs, Flasks, Vials, Syringes (and most consumables) store 3 per slot. Remedies take a full slot each.
- **Weapon qualities:** Hefty (one-handed; can't hold two) · Bulky (extra slot; two hands if a weapon) · Long (Impaired in tight spaces; two hands) · Slow (no move and attack with it in one turn) · Fixed (needs a turn to set up) · Hidden (easy to conceal) · plus range qualities.

## 11. Rest and Recovery
- **Short Rest** (minutes): restores Guard, removes Fatigue.
- **Night's Rest** (safe place, food, lodging): restores Vigor to full.
- **Full Rest** (a week, safe): restores all Virtues.
- **Remedies:** an hour, serve up to 4 Fatekin, restore 6 points of their Virtue (Stitcher-Drone 6 Vigor, Stimulant-Rig 6 Grace, Sedative-Assortment 6 Spirit).
- **Impassioned:** a resource earned by indulging your Passion, great roleplay, or cleverness. Spend one to automatically pass a Save that would cause Fatigue from a Feat, or to restore 1d6 Spirit.

## 12. Structures and Vehicles
- Structures and Vehicles at 0 Guard are **Wrecked** (all inside take d6 Damage). Unsuitable weapons are Impaired against them. They have no Virtues and don't Save; the Pilot saves with a sensible Virtue (usually Grace).
- A Vehicle's Move is its **Speed** in zones. Colliding Vehicles both take d6 to Guard (heavier gets +d12). While piloted, Feats can be used as if the Pilot were attacking.
- **Battle-Vehicles** multiply all damage dice by 5 against small organic targets.
- Examples: Pedalcycle (Guard 3, Armor 1, 1 Zone), Magecycle (6, 1, 2), Mage Carriage (8, 2, 2), Luxury Mage Carriage (10, 2, 3), GA-Charger (8, 3, 2 Zones), GA-Crawler (10, 3, 1), GA-Cruiser (18, 4, 1).

## House style notes (for Claude)
- Always use Touch/Close/Medium/Long-Range (Touch = Engaged, Close = your zone, Medium = adjacent zones in sight, Long = line of sight), Blast (Small)/(Large), Virtue Loss (not "direct damage"), and Engaged as defined above.
- Rolling low is good: a d20 Save passes when equal to or under the Virtue.
- When balancing, note which rules a change touches (Engaged, Guard, Blast size) and flag conflicts rather than silently resolving them.
